## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Introibo is a prayer companion built for traditional Catholics. Designed for the 1962 Roman Missal and Roman Breviary, it puts the entire liturgical day at your fingertips — ad-free, offline, and without subscription.

YOUR DAILY COMPANION
The Hodie screen shows today's feast, the proper of the Mass and Divine Office, fasting and abstinence obligations, and the appropriate Marian antiphon — all reflecting authentic 1962 rubrics. Follow a patron saint with daily streak tracking.

THE 1962 MISSAL
The complete Ordinary of the Mass woven together with 428 daily Propers in correct order. Latin and English side-by-side, with 14 proper Prefaces for every season and feast, proper Canon insertions for Christmas, Easter, Ascension, and Pentecost, Tracts for Septuagesima through Palm Sunday, and the full Sacred Triduum. Gloria and Credo appear only when the rubrics prescribe; Psalm 42 is omitted in Passiontide; the correct dismissal (Ite Missa Est, Benedicamus Domino, or Requiescant in pace) is chosen automatically.

THE DIVINE OFFICE
Pray all 8 canonical hours of the 1962 Roman Breviary at the proper time. Class I and II feasts correctly receive 3-nocturn Matins with Te Deum; ferial Office stays compact. Short responsories at Prime, Terce, Sext, and None. Marian antiphons with versicles and collects follow the liturgical season. Every prayer — Pater Noster, Ave Maria, Credo, Confiteor — is spelled out in full.

40 TRADITIONAL PRAYERS
A personal prayer rule for morning, midday, and evening. 12 occasion categories including During Mass, Before Confession, Marian, Eucharistic, and more. Plus the complete Rosary (Joyful, Sorrowful, Glorious), Stations of the Cross with Tiepolo's 1749 engravings, and an examination of conscience for Confession.

LEARN
10 Latin lessons with Ecclesiastical pronunciation, 97 flashcards, daily vocabulary, diphthongs, declensions, and verb basics. Master the Ave Maria and Pater Noster word by word.

REFERENCE LIBRARY
41 articles, 428 searchable Propers, a history of the Mass timeline, and a glossary of liturgical terms.

SETTINGS
Switch Missal rite (1962, 1955, or pre-1955), penance discipline, language preference (Latin only, English only, or both), theme (parchment, clean white, dark), and text size. English Only mode changes everything to English across the entire app.

NO ADS. NO TRACKING. NO SUBSCRIPTION.
Everything works offline. No internet permission. No analytics. Your prayer life stays on your device.

Ad altare Dei.
