About

Introibo is a free iOS app for Catholics who attend the Traditional Latin Mass. It puts the complete 1962 Missale Romanum in your pocket with 422 daily Propers automatically interleaved with the Ordinary in correct liturgical order, along with 30 traditional prayers, an interactive Rosary, Stations of the Cross, the Divine Office, a confession guide, Latin lessons, and a reference library.

The app works entirely offline. No accounts, no tracking, no ads. All data is stored locally on your device.

Features

Complete interleaved Missal with daily Propers for every day of the liturgical year. 30 prayers in side-by-side Latin and English. Bead-by-bead Rosary with Joyful, Sorrowful, and Glorious mysteries. 14 Stations of the Cross with meditations. All 8 canonical hours of the 1962 Breviary. Examination of Conscience with guided confession paths. 10 Latin lessons with 91 flashcards and quizzes. 41 reference articles. 7 patron saints with daily practices.

Privacy

Introibo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. All app data, including settings, prayer history, and lesson progress, is stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. Nothing leaves your device.

The app does not use encryption and does not connect to the internet for any purpose. It works completely offline.
