# Store copy — v1.2.3 (Android 1.2.3 / iOS 1.5.7)

## App Store promotional text (166/170 chars)

New: Home Screen widgets for the feast, the Office, and daily prayers — plus a year-view calendar and deeply audited 1962 Missal and Breviary texts. Ad-free, offline.

## App Store "What's New"

WIDGETS
Bring the liturgical day to your Home Screen: Today's Feast, a Daily
Reading, the Divine Office hour by hour, your chosen prayers for morning,
midday, and evening, and a Sanctorale widget showing where you are in the
Church's year and the saints ahead. Small and large sizes are each designed
for their space, styled like a missal page — ribbon markers, drop caps, and
the day's liturgical color.

THE YEAR AT A GLANCE
- A new year overview in the calendar: the seasons as bands, with a "you
  are here" marker.
- Jump straight to the moveable feasts — Easter, Ascension, Pentecost,
  Corpus Christi, Christ the King.
- Month cells now mark vigils, octave days, Ember days, and days of penance.
- An optional Upcoming Feasts card on the Home screen (off by default —
  enable it in Settings).

THE MOST ACCURATE TEXTS WE HAVE EVER SHIPPED
We audited every day of the liturgical year against primary sources — every
hour of the Breviary and every Mass, in all three supported rubrics (1962,
1955, pre-1955):
- The Divine Office now renders feast and commemoration propers in full:
  the correct ferial Matins psalter, proper psalm assignments at Matins,
  Lauds, and Vespers, the commune responsories, the Great O antiphons, the
  Triduum collects, and the Roman (not monastic) Vespers structure.
- The Canon of the Mass is complete on the great feasts: the proper
  Communicantes no longer truncates, the 1962 Canon adds the St. Joseph
  clause, the dismissal precedes the Placeat, and the communion of the
  faithful includes the Ecce Agnus Dei.
- Sequences restored (Victimae paschali, Stabat Mater, Dies irae), Trinity
  Sunday's introit corrected, Holy Name Sunday's Mass and Office repaired,
  and hundreds of oration and calendar corrections.
- New prayer: the Litany of the Sacred Heart.

Also in this release: text-size accessibility improvements throughout.

Ad altare Dei.
